## How the game differs from other one-tap orbit games
- **Skim system:** flying close past a mine (without touching it) scores a bonus and builds a streak, so risky lines are rewarded and the score multiplier goes up to x6.
- **Planet types:** fast-spinning planets and crumbling planets that throw you off after a couple of seconds.
- **Slow-motion orbs:** collecting one slows time for a moment, so you can line up a hard shot.
- **Melody:** every hop plays the next note of a pentatonic scale, so a good run sounds like a tune.
- **Rising pressure:** the view climbs steadily and speeds up, so waiting is never safe.

## Accessibility tags (AGI): tag only what is true
- Single-input play (tap, click or Space); no multi-touch or precision gestures.
- Audio is feedback only; the game never requires sound and follows YouTube's mute.
- Respects the system "reduce motion" setting (less shake and fewer particles).
- NOT true, do not tag: no flashing-free guarantee has been audited, and there is no keyboard-only, screen reader or colour-blind support.
- The game is real-time and gets faster, so do not tag it as "no time pressure".

## Preview video (generated by `node store/record_run.mjs && python3 store/make_video.py`, needs ffmpeg)
`store/nova-skim-preview-1920x1080.mp4`: 16:9, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps, 12 s, H.264 + AAC stereo, about -15 LUFS (about 2 MB).
It is a real playthrough: `record_run.mjs` plays `src/game/world.js` with a bot, so every hop, skim and orb follows the game's
actual rules. Side captions ("TAP TO RELEASE", "SKIM MINES FOR COMBOS", "GRAB ORBS FOR SLOW-MO") are timed to real events.
No logos or branding text. The soundtrack (`make_audio.py`) is generated from math: the game's own tones at each event
plus a soft arpeggio, so there are no samples and no music rights to clear.
